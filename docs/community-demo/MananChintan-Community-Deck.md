# MananChintan — Community presentation (slide script)

**Audience:** Community members (~150)  
**Presenter goal:** Show how to use the app day-to-day in **5–10 minutes**, then Q&A.  
**Live URL:** _Add your production link here (e.g. `https://gunanuvad.vercel.app`)._

---

## Slide 1 — Title

**MananChintan**  
A simple app for our community — daily notes, reflections, photos, and standings.

_Speaker notes:_ Introduce yourself in one sentence. Say this is a **web app** that works in the browser and can be **saved to the home screen** like an app (PWA).

---

## Slide 2 — What you use it for

- **Home:** your day — greeting, community spotlight, roster & picks  
- **Feed:** everyone’s **Smruti** photo posts in one timeline  
- **Standings:** points and streaks  
- **Swadhyay:** weekly reflection on the current topic  
- **Smruti:** share **1–5 photos** with a **caption** (your “moments”)

_Speaker notes:_ Emphasize **respectful, satsang-friendly** use. No need to name every tab yet — next slide shows where they live.

---

## Slide 3 — How to open it

1. Open the link we share (or scan a QR code if you print one).  
2. **Sign in with Google** the first time.  
3. _(Optional)_ **Add to Home Screen** on iPhone/Android for a full-screen icon.

_Speaker notes:_ Demo on projector: open the real URL. If people struggle, mention “bookmark the page.”

---

## Slide 4 — Bottom navigation (five tabs)

**Home · Feed · Standings · Swadhyay · Smruti**

- The **orange** tab is where you are now.  
- You can switch any time; your work is **saved on your account**.

_Speaker notes:_ Hold phone up or show `screenshots/02-bottom-nav.png`.

---

## Slide 5 — Home

- **Jay Swaminarayan** greeting with your name  
- **Community Spotlight:** rotating mix of **approved notes (ghun)**, **Feed photos**, and **recent Swadhyay** (last 7 days)  
- **Roster / picks** for the day’s campaign (if your sabha uses it)

_Speaker notes:_ This is the “hub.” Don’t deep-dive roster rules unless your audience needs it.

---

## Slide 6 — Community Spotlight (why it matters)

- Celebrates **each other** — notes, reflections, and moments in **one small carousel**  
- Same **medium-sized** card for every type so nothing feels broken on phones

_Speaker notes:_ Reinforce positivity — “we lift each other up.”

---

## Slide 7 — Feed (everyone’s Smruti)

- Open **Feed** to see **all members’** Smruti posts  
- Scroll, **like** once (likes stay — no “unlike”)  
- Authors and organizers can **delete** a post if needed

_Speaker notes:_ Mention **photos are compressed** when posting so the app stays fast for everyone.

---

## Slide 8 — Smruti (share a moment)

1. Tap **Smruti**.  
2. Add **1–5 photos** (library or camera where supported).  
3. Write a **caption** (required).  
4. Tap **Post** — it appears on **Feed** for everyone.

_Speaker notes:_ Show composer + success on Feed. Mention **JPEG / PNG / WebP / GIF**, reasonable size limits.

---

## Slide 9 — Swadhyay (weekly reflection)

- Each **topic** is set for a period; you post your **reflection**  
- You can **like** and **reply** where enabled  
- Stay kind and on-topic

_Speaker notes:_ If only sabha leads post topics, say that clearly.

---

## Slide 10 — Standings

- **Points** and **streaks** for eligible activities  
- Encouragement, not judgment — follow sabha guidance on how scores are used

_Speaker notes:_ Calibrate tone to your group’s culture.

---

## Slide 11 — Profile menu (top-right avatar)

- **View full profile** (`/me`)  
- **Calendar** (if your sabha uses it)  
- **Dark mode** / **Notifications** toggles  
- **Sign out** when on a shared device

_Speaker notes:_ **Admin** only for organizers — skip unless this slide is for leaders only.

---

## Slide 12 — Tips for a smooth experience

- **One Google account** per person avoids split progress.  
- **Notifications:** turn on if you want day reminders (system + in-app where applicable).  
- **Shared phone?** Always **sign out** from Profile.

---

## Slide 13 — Storage & kindness (optional 30 seconds)

- The app is built for **~150 people for a season** — please avoid dumping huge galleries in one post.  
- **Organizers** may remove content that breaks sabha rules.

_Speaker notes:_ Optional; skip if time is tight.
