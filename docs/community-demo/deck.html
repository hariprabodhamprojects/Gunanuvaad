What it is

One app for our sabha season

  • Home — your day, community spotlight, roster
  • Feed — everyone’s Smruti photo posts
  • Standings — points & streaks
  • Swadhyay — weekly reflection
  • Smruti — share 1–5 photos with a caption

Getting started

Open the link & sign in

  • Open the sabha link in the phone browser (or scan a QR code).
  • Tap Continue with Google and pick your account.
  • Optional: Add to Home Screen for a full-screen icon (PWA).

Feed

Everyone’s Smruti posts

  • Open Feed to scroll the shared timeline.
  • Like once per post (likes stay).
  • Authors & organizers can delete a post when needed.
screenshots/05-feed.png

Smruti

Share a moment

  1. Tap Smruti.
  2. Add 1–5 photos.
  3. Write a caption (required).
  4. Tap Post — it appears on Feed.
